# Genesis Prestige — User Manual

Welcome. This manual is for SACCO staff who use the Genesis Prestige
management console day to day: tellers, loan officers, accountants, branch
managers, committee members, auditors and administrators.

It is written in plain language. Each chapter walks you through real tasks,
step by step. You do not need any technical background.

> ℹ️ Some actions described here may be hidden or disabled for you. What you
> can see and do depends on the role your administrator gave you. Chapter 10
> explains roles.

## Words we use

- **Member** — a person, company, group or vehicle owner who belongs to the
  SACCO.
- **Member number** — the unique number every member gets at registration,
  for example GP-0007. Lists often show a member as
  "Jane Wanjiku · GP-0007" so you always see the name and the number
  together.
- **Posting** — recording a money movement (a deposit, a withdrawal, a
  repayment) in the system.
- **Proposer and approver** — for sensitive actions, one person prepares the
  action and a *different* person approves it. This manual explains it the
  first time it appears (Chapter 6) and uses "proposer and approver" from
  then on.
- **Approval limit** — the largest amount your role is allowed to approve.
  Amounts above your limit need someone with a higher limit.
